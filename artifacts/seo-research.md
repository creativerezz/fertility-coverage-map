## 2026-10-20

**New findings:**
- DMHC issued revised APL 25-021 (Oct 5, 2026) clarifying SB 729 implementation. Revisions apply to contracts issued, amended, or renewed on or after July 1, 2027. Key points include definitions (completed retrieval, donor, etc.), covered services list (diagnosis, treatment, donor/surrogate, cryopreservation 5 years large-group / 1 year small-group offer), lifetime limits rules, and notices.
- Blue Shield of California updated SB 729 External FAQs (Oct 13, 2026) acknowledging the revised APL, noting they are reviewing impacts, and that any benefit changes require DMHC approval. Current 2026 approved benefits remain in place for plans before July 2027.
- Federal proposed rule (May 13, 2026; REG-118484-25) would create "excepted fertility benefits" category (up to $120k lifetime per participant, indexed), allowing standalone fertility coverage (including for self-funded plans) exempt from many ACA/ERISA market rules, for plan years beginning on/after Jan 1, 2027. Comments closed; not yet finalized.
- No new enacted state mandates, major bill progress, or court rulings specifically altering fertility insurance coverage identified in recent searches. Existing 2026 effective mandates (CA SB 729, plus FL/GA/NV references) and pending items (e.g., Virginia for 2028) remain.
